# 2026-08-12

A friend dropped off a small bag of beans, no label beyond a handwritten note: "for your
collection, 🧑‍🌾 grown nearby, my grandmother calls it plain old good coffee." I do not
know the exact origin, so this stays out of [[Beans/Ethiopia Yirgacheffe|the catalog]] until I do.

Brewed a cup on a medium-fine grind, see [[Brewing/Grind Size Guide#Pour Over]], using the usual
[[Brewing/Pour Over Basics|pour-over]] method. Tasted rounder than the
[[Beans/Sumatra Mandheling]] from two days ago, less funky, more like a well-made café
pour over.

One small note for later, unrelated to taste: I am spelling "café" two ways on purpose
in this vault. Both are the letter e with an acute accent, only the encoding differs:
café is one composed character, and café is a plain e followed by a
separate combining accent mark. They should render identically even though they are different
bytes.

Also logging 🫘 here as a placeholder marker: whatever this bean turns out to be, it
earns its own note once I can identify where it came from.
