# Kettle

A gooseneck kettle with a variable-temperature base. Used for every method except
[[Brewing/Cold Brew|cold brew]], which does not use heat at all.

## Typical temperatures

- Light roasts such as [[Beans/Ethiopia Yirgacheffe]]: closer to boiling.
- Dark roasts such as [[Beans/Sumatra Mandheling]]: a little cooler, to avoid emphasizing
  bitterness that the roast already carries.
