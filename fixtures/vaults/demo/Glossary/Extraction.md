# Extraction

Extraction is how much soluble material moves out of the ground coffee and into the water. Every
brew method in [[Brewing/Grind Size Guide]] is really a way of controlling this one variable
through grind size, time, temperature, and agitation.

## Under-Extraction

Not enough dissolves. The cup tastes sour, salty, or thin, and often finishes abruptly. Usually
fixed with a finer grind, more time, or hotter water.

## Over-Extraction

Too much dissolves, including bitter, less desirable compounds that come out later in the
extraction. The cup tastes bitter, dry, or hollow. Usually fixed with a coarser grind, less time,
or cooler water.

See [[Glossary/Vocabulaire du café]] for the same ideas in French.
