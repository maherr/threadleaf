# Colombia Huila

Medium roast, washed process. The reliable everyday bean: balanced, a little caramel sweetness,
forgiving of an imprecise grind.

## Notes

Works well across methods. Good in the [[Brewing/AeroPress|AeroPress]] for a fast morning cup and
in [[Brewing/Espresso Ratios|espresso]] without much dialing in.

Rated highest of the three beans currently in stock; see [[Beans/Ethiopia Yirgacheffe]] and
[[Beans/Sumatra Mandheling]] for the others.
