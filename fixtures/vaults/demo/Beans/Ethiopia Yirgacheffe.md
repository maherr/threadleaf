# Ethiopia Yirgacheffe

Light roast, washed process. Floral and citrus-forward, the kind of bean that rewards a careful
[[Brewing/Pour Over Basics|pour over]] more than a [[Brewing/French Press|French press]].

## Notes

Bright acidity, jasmine and lemon on the nose, clean finish. Fades noticeably after about three
weeks off the roast date, so buy what will actually get used.

## Where it fits

Small batches only, tracked against [[Projects/Equipment Wishlist|the wishlist]] budget alongside
equipment, since good single-origin beans are not free either.
