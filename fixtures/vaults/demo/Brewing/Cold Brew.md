# Cold Brew

A long, cold, full-immersion steep. See [[Brewing/Grind Size Guide#Cold Brew]] for grind size.

## Ratio

1:8 for a concentrate that gets diluted with water or milk before drinking, roughly double the
strength of a normal [[Brewing/French Press|French press]] cup.

## Steps

1. Combine extra-coarse grounds and room-temperature or cold water.
2. Steep 12 to 18 hours at room temperature or in the refrigerator.
3. Strain through a fine mesh or paper filter.
4. Dilute to taste before serving.

Best made with a mellow, low-acid bean; [[Beans/Sumatra Mandheling]] works well here.
