# AeroPress

A small, forgiving immersion brewer. See [[Brewing/Grind Size Guide#AeroPress]] for grind size.

## Ratio

1:14 for something closer to drip strength, or as low as 1:10 for a concentrate meant to be
diluted like the base of a [[Brewing/Cold Brew|cold brew]].

## Steps

1. Add grounds and hot water, stir for 10 seconds.
2. Steep 1 to 2 minutes.
3. Press steadily over 20 to 30 seconds.

Short steep time makes this the fastest way to test a new bag from [[Beans/Colombia Huila]] or a
similar bean without committing a full pot.
