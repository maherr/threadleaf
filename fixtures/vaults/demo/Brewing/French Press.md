# French Press

A full-immersion method. See [[Brewing/Grind Size Guide#French Press]] for grind size.

## Ratio

1:15 is a reasonable starting point, a little stronger than [[Brewing/Pour Over Basics|pour over]].

## Steps

1. Add coarse grounds to the empty carafe.
2. Pour all the water at once and stir gently.
3. Steep for 4 minutes, then break the crust and skim the foam.
4. Press slowly and pour immediately so the grounds stop steeping.

Leaving coffee sitting on the grounds after pressing is the most common mistake; it keeps
extracting and turns bitter within minutes. See [[Glossary/Extraction#Over-Extraction]].
