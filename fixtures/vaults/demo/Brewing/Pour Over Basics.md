# Pour Over Basics

A manual method that trades speed for control. See [[Brewing/Grind Size Guide#Pour Over]] for
grind size.

## Ratio

Start at 1:16, coffee to water by weight, and adjust from there. See
[[Brewing/Espresso Ratios|the ratio note]] for how this compares to espresso.

## Steps

1. Rinse the filter and preheat the [[Equipment/Kettle|kettle]] water.
2. Bloom with twice the coffee's weight in water for 30 to 45 seconds.
3. Pour the remaining water in two or three slow passes.
4. Total brew time lands around 3 to 4 minutes.

#pour-over technique improves faster with a consistent kettle and a [[Equipment/Scale|scale]] than
with a better grinder.
