# Espresso Ratios

Espresso measures a much narrower ratio window than the immersion and pour-over methods, because a
few seconds of extra contact time changes the cup dramatically. See
[[Brewing/Grind Size Guide#Espresso]] for grind size.

![[Attachments/brew-ratio-chart.png|Espresso ratio chart]]

## Reading the chart

- Ristretto stops the shot early, around 1:1.5.
- Espresso is the common middle ground, around 1:2.
- Lungo runs longer, around 1:3, and is more exposed to over-extraction.

Getting this wrong in either direction produces one of the two failure modes below, transcluded
from the shared glossary entry:

![[Glossary/Extraction#Over-Extraction]]

Under-extraction is the more common beginner mistake; see [[Glossary/Extraction#Under-Extraction]]
for the same detail without embedding it here twice.
