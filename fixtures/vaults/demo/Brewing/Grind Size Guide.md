# Grind Size Guide

A quick reference for matching grind size to brew method. Coarser grinds suit longer contact
times; finer grinds suit shorter, pressurized contact times.

## Espresso

Fine, close to powdered sugar. Used by [[Brewing/Espresso Ratios]]. Too coarse and the shot runs
fast and thin; too fine and it chokes the machine.

## Pour Over

Medium, close to sea salt. Used by [[Brewing/Pour Over Basics]] and by whatever
[[Journal/2026-08-12|today's mystery bean]] turns into.

## French Press

Coarse, close to breadcrumbs. Used by [[Brewing/French Press]]. Fine grounds slip past the mesh
filter and end up in the cup.

## AeroPress

Medium-fine, adjustable by steep time. Used by [[Brewing/AeroPress]].

## Cold Brew

Extra coarse, close to peppercorns. Used by [[Brewing/Cold Brew]]. A long steep time makes fine
particles over-extract and turn bitter; see [[Glossary/Extraction#Over-Extraction]].
